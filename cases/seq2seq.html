Predicting what might happen next.

Event-sequence prediction using a BiLSTM encoder–decoder and simulator data, with careful tuning and evaluation of sequences that may have different lengths.

01 / The question

How can a model learn from a sequence of events when both the event order and sequence length matter?

While working as a research assistant, I trained a sequence-to-sequence neural network on multivariate simulator events. The encoder studies what has happened; the decoder estimates what may happen next. Instead of relying on a single training setting, I explored comparisons and tuning.

02 / What the evidence showed

What was difficult—and what I learned.

Masking and careful evaluation mattered because padded positions in variable-length sequences should not count as real events. Through this project I learned to compare runs, manage checkpoints, and distinguish improvements on a known setup from a general claim that a model will work everywhere.
