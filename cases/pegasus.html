01 / The problem

How do we shorten a dialogue without losing its meaning?

A useful summary is more than a shorter version of the input. It should keep the details that matter and avoid adding facts. I used the SAMSum dataset to adapt a PEGASUS summarization model, building a modular training pipeline from the original research experiments.

The project helped me understand how tokenization, fine-tuning, evaluation and model serving fit together. A sample dialogue may be shortened to a single sentence, but real summaries still need checking. The repository contains training and inference code; it is not presented as a currently hosted production service.
