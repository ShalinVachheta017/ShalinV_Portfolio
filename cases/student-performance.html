Student Performance Prediction

I compared nine regression models on the same held-out split to estimate math scores. Ridge had the lowest RMSE at 5.39 score points, with ordinary linear regression close behind.

ModelR²RMSE

Both linear models outperformed Random Forest and XGBoost on this split. Ridge can stabilise coefficients when predictors are correlated. The small difference between the two linear models leaves the reason for Ridge’s lead open.
