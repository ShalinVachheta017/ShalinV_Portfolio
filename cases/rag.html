Finding the right evidence matters more than writing a fluent answer.

A multilingual tender-document assistant for finding passages in long collections of German procurement files. The interesting engineering problem was retrieval: what happens when the document is there, but search misses it?

01 / The question

How can a local assistant answer a practical question using a specific collection of documents, without pretending its own words are evidence?

I worked with a documented collection of 6,126 procurement documents. The workflow extracts and prepares text, stores searchable representations, searches by meaning or metadata, and passes retrieved passages to a local language model. Users can ask questions in English or German even though the documents are mainly German.

02 / What the evidence showed

What was difficult—and what I learned.

The hard cases were not just long documents. Exact-ID lookup and cross-language questions revealed weaknesses in search. That changed how I thought about AI answers: the retrieval step deserves at least as much attention as the text generation step. A confident-looking answer can still be unhelpful if the evidence was missed.
